On your Mac.

On-device speech with AI cleanup switched off keeps your audio and text on your Mac. No per-call API charges.

Or through a provider.

Use your own OpenRouter or ElevenLabs key. Cloud speech sends audio to your selected provider. Dictionary hints go to Grok, Gemini one-step and ElevenLabs. Gemini one-step also receives replacement rules, even with cleanup off. Optional AI cleanup sends your transcript, app name and dictionary to OpenRouter. Provider charges or credits apply.

Install from source

One command.
Your Mac.

There is no signed download yet. Until there is, Mercurius builds on your Mac and signs itself with your own free Apple certificate. It ships with no keys and no accounts; you add your own.

  1. An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later.
  2. Xcode from the App Store, opened once.
  3. A free Apple Development certificate: Xcode › Settings › Accounts › Manage Certificates › +.
git clone https://github.com/SHAMALKRISHNA/Mercurius.git
cd Mercurius
sh scripts/install.sh

It builds Mercurius, puts it in your Applications folder and opens it. Run it again to update; the old copy goes to your Trash. Then allow the microphone and Accessibility, and add an OpenRouter or ElevenLabs key, or use on-device speech with no key at all.
