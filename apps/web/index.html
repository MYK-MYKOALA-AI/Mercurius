Mercurius puts the words into the app you’re using, so you can stay with the thought.

01 / Dictation

Write where you work.

Notes, emails, messages. Use your dictation shortcut in the app you have open.

02 / Your vocabulary

Keep the names right.

Add the people, projects and words you use. Save phrases you find yourself repeating.

03 / Optional cleanup

Leave the ums behind.

Choose whether to tidy filler words and punctuation. Set a writing style for each app.

Your Mac. Your choice.

Decide where
the words go.

On your Mac.

On-device speech with AI cleanup switched off keeps your audio and text on your Mac. No per-call API charges.

Or through a provider.

Use your own OpenRouter or ElevenLabs key. Cloud speech sends audio to your selected provider. Grok and ElevenLabs also receive dictionary hints. Optional AI cleanup sends your transcript, app name and dictionary to OpenRouter. Provider charges or credits apply.

Mercurius is being built as a free, open-source alternative to paid dictation apps, with no required app subscription. The application source is public under the MIT licence. Read it, build it, or help improve it on GitHub. The app is still in testing; there is no verified public installer yet.
